What We Liked

Smooth gas-spring height adjustment, per owner reports, with good stability once mounted and tension set.

Both C-clamp and grommet mounts are included, so it fits desks with or without a grommet hole.

The 22 lb capacity is the highest of the three monitor arms we review, giving more headroom for heavier 27 inch panels.

What Could Be Better

Assembly instructions could be clearer, according to owners — allow extra time on the first install.

Max 22 lb still limits use with heavy professional monitors, and the stated 32 inch size maximum rules out larger panels.

Slightly plastic-heavy feel compared to pricier arms, per owner feedback.

Performance in Daily Use

Owner reports describe a stable hold on standard 24 to 27 inch monitors with no drift once the gas spring is tensioned for the panel's weight. The lift is repeatedly described as smooth; the recurring caveats are the install instructions and the plastic-heavy build feel.

Who This Is For

Buyers who want gas-spring adjustability for a standard 24 to 27 inch monitor, especially a heavier panel where the 22 lb capacity gives useful headroom over 20 lb arms. Running a wider panel? Our ultrawide monitor arm guide runs the weight and headroom numbers on this arm against 34-inch panels — including where its stated 32-inch size range becomes the limit.

Bottom Line

The HUANUO Single Monitor Arm is the capacity pick among the budget gas-spring arms we review: 22 lb headroom, both mounts included, and the standard four adjustments. For a 34 inch ultrawide, check our ultrawide guide first — the stated 32 inch maximum is the limit that matters.
