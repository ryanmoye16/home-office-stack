What We Liked

Smooth gas-spring height adjustment. Good stability once mounted. Both C-clamp and grommet included. 5-year warranty is rare at this price.

What Could Be Better

Assembly instructions could be clearer. Max 22 lbs limits use with heavy professional monitors. Slightly plastic heavy feel compared to pricier arms.

Tested on a 27" BenQ PD2700U (14 lbs) — stable with no drift over 3 weeks. Gas spring is smooth.

Who This Is For

Buyers who want gas-spring adjustability without spending $80+. Good for standard 24–27" monitors.

Bottom Line
