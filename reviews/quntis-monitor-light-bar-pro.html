Short version

Buy if you work in variable light on a 15–32 inch monitor and want auto-dimming plus a wireless rotary remote to dial brightness and color temperature (2700K–6500K) from the desk instead of reaching up to the bar.

Skip if your monitor is a 34-inch-plus ultrawide (owners report uneven coverage at the edges), you have no nearby USB port for its short fixed cable, or a wireless remote does not matter to you — the standard Quntis covers the same core light-bar job with touch controls.

What We Liked

Owners consistently praise the auto-dimming: the ambient light sensor adjusts brightness as room light changes, so the desk stays evenly lit without repeated manual adjustment.

The wireless rotary remote is the other recurring positive. Owner feedback describes dialing brightness and color temperature from the desk rather than reaching up to the bar, which is the main step up over the standard model's touch controls.

Owner feedback also reports the asymmetric optic landing light on the desk and keyboard rather than the screen, and color temperature adjusting across the catalog range from warm 2700K to cool 6500K for morning versus late-night work.

What Could Be Better

The USB power cable is fixed and relatively short, a recurring owner complaint — plan on a nearby USB port or a wall adapter.

Some owners on larger ultrawide monitors (34-inch+) report the bar does not cover the full width evenly — the catalog fit is monitors 15–32 inches, so wider screens may see uneven coverage at the edges.

The remote uses a coin cell battery (included) rather than being rechargeable, so it will need replacing over time.

Performance in Daily Use

Owner feedback on the PRO+ centers on two things: the auto-dimming sensor responding to changing room light (window light, overhead lights, lamps) without manual intervention, and the absence of screen glare from the asymmetric optic at working brightness levels.

Those reports are consistent across long-term owner reviews of this model; the recurring cautions are the short fixed cable and uneven coverage on monitors wider than the bar is rated for, both covered above.

Who This Is For

Anyone who works in a variable-light environment and wants a monitor light bar with auto-dimming plus a wireless remote for brightness and color control. It is especially good for evening workers who want warmer light at night. If a wireless remote is not important, the Quntis Monitor Light Bar (Standard) review covers the touch-control model in this same line.

Bottom Line

The Quntis Monitor Light Bar PRO+ covers the core light-bar job — auto-dimming, a wide color-temperature range, and light aimed at the desk instead of the screen — and adds a wireless rotary remote as its step-up feature, with a short cable as the honest compromise.
