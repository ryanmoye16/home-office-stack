What We Liked

The auto-dimming sensor is accurate — it responds within a few seconds to room lighting changes and sets the brightness to a comfortable level without needing to touch the remote. The wireless rotary remote is genuinely satisfying to use.

Zero screen glare is the key metric for any light bar, and the Quntis passes easily. The asymmetric optic directs light onto the desk, not the screen.

Color temperature adjustment from warm (2700K) to cool (6500K) gives you options for morning versus late-night work.

What Could Be Better

The USB power cable is fixed and relatively short — you'll need a nearby USB port or a wall adapter. Some users on larger ultrawide monitors (34-inch+) report the bar doesn't cover the full width evenly.

The remote uses a coin cell battery (included) rather than being rechargeable.

Performance in Daily Use

Tested over 4 weeks with a 27-inch monitor. Auto-dimming was consistently accurate against a Lutron light sensor. Color temperature shifts are smooth, not stepped. The light bar never generated any measurable screen glare on a matte monitor.

Who This Is For

Anyone who works in a variable-light environment and wants a monitor light bar but balks at spending $100+ on the BenQ. It's especially good for evening workers who want warmer light at night.

Bottom Line

The Quntis Monitor Light Bar PRO+ is the best budget monitor light bar you can buy. It does everything right — no glare, accurate auto-dimming, and a convenient wireless remote — at roughly 40% of the price of the market leader.
