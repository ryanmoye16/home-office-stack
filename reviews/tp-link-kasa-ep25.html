What We Liked

Matter support means it works with every major smart home platform without being locked to one ecosystem. Energy monitoring is genuinely useful — you can see exactly how much power your devices are drawing and identify energy hogs. The Kasa app is reliable and the scheduling works correctly.

The price for a 2-pack makes this exceptional value. Each plug is compact enough that you can fit two in a standard duplex outlet without blocking the second socket.

What Could Be Better

No thread or Zigbee — purely Wi-Fi. If your router is far from the plug, connectivity can be inconsistent. The physical design is slightly bulkier than some competitors.

The energy monitoring requires the Kasa app — it doesn't export data to Home Assistant easily without workarounds.

Performance in Daily Use

Reliable operation over 3 months of testing. Schedules fired on time, energy monitoring tracked accurately against a kill-a-watt meter, and voice commands via Alexa responded within 1-2 seconds.

Who This Is For

Anyone building a Matter smart home who wants energy monitoring without a subscription. Also excellent for people who want to track and reduce energy costs on high-draw devices like space heaters or desktop equipment.

Bottom Line

The TP-Link Kasa EP25 2-pack is the smart plug to buy if you want Matter compatibility and energy monitoring without a subscription fee. Solid, reliable, and well-priced.
