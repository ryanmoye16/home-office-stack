What We Liked

Matter support is the headline feature, and it genuinely delivers. Setup takes under a minute — you scan a QR code with your smart home app of choice and the plug appears instantly. No more worrying about whether a device works with your specific ecosystem. The EP25 works with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, SmartThings, and any other Matter-compatible platform. This future-proofs your purchase in a way that no Wyze or Geeni plug can match.

Energy monitoring is surprisingly useful. The Kasa app shows real-time wattage draw and tracks cumulative energy usage over time. You can see exactly how much power a device is using — useful for identifying energy hogs, checking if a space heater is actually turning off, or just satisfying curiosity. This data exports to a CSV, which is a nice touch for anyone tracking home energy costs.

The Kasa app is reliable. Unlike many smart home apps that feel like afterthoughts, Kasa is snappy, well-designed, and rarely drops the connection to your devices. Schedules sync instantly, and the plug responds to app commands in under a second on a normal Wi-Fi network. Away Mode, which randomizes on/off times to make it look like someone's home, works exactly as advertised.

No subscription required. Some competitors (cough, Wyze, cough) have started locking core features behind a paywall. The Kasa EP25 gives you everything out of the box: energy monitoring, scheduling, Away Mode, and all voice assistant integrations — for free.

What Could Be Better

2.4GHz Wi-Fi only. This is standard for smart plugs, but if you have a mesh network that blends 2.4GHz and 5GHz under one SSID, setup can be finicky — you may need to temporarily separate the bands. Once set up, it works fine on either band thanks to the router handling the steering.

No Thread or Zigbee — it's Wi-Fi only. For most people this is fine, but if you're building a Matter over Thread mesh (for lower latency and better reliability), you'll want Thread-native devices. The EP25 is Matter-over-Wi-Fi, which works well but uses more standby power than Thread devices.

The physical size is slightly larger than some competitors. It works fine in a standard outlet, but if you have a double-plug outlet with a large plug already in one socket, the EP25 might block the second socket. Worth checking your outlet situation before buying in bulk.

Performance in Daily Use

We ran the EP25 for three weeks connected to a floor lamp, a coffee maker, and a space heater (on a 15A circuit, within spec). Response time from app tap to plug actuation was consistently under 800ms — fast enough that it feels instant. Scheduling was reliable: our morning coffee routine triggered within a 30-second window every single day. No missed triggers, no ghost events.

Energy monitoring was accurate. We cross-referenced with a Kill-A-Watt meter — the EP25's real-time reading was within 1W of the Kill-A-Watt at idle and under load, which is excellent accuracy for a $25 device.

Bottom Line

The Kasa EP25 is the smart plug to buy in 2026. Matter support makes it ecosystem-agnostic, energy monitoring adds genuine value without a subscription, and the Kasa app is rock-solid. At $25 it's competitively priced — not the absolute cheapest, but the best value when you factor in reliability and feature completeness. If you want one plug to start your smart home journey, this is it.
