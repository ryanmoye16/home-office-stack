What We Liked

The finished, leather-look surface is the draw — reviewers consistently call out how clean and refined it makes a desk look, in person and on camera. The smooth PU leather lets a mouse glide freely, and tester reports credit it with precise tracking for detailed work. It lies flat right away, with no curled edges to fight. The water-resistant surface wipes clean with a damp cloth and protects the desk underneath from spills and scratches.

What Could Be Better

At 0.1 inch thick it is a surface, not a cushion — wrists get little padding compared with a thicker mat. It comes in a single size, so desks that need wider or deeper coverage have to look elsewhere. The smooth backing is the recurring complaint in reviews: with no non-slip grip, the mat can nudge around during heavy typing. Scratch-resistant is not scratch-proof, either — Satechi's own care guidance warns against sharp objects and harsh cleaners on the PU surface.

Performance in Daily Use

Reviewer and owner reports focus on glide and upkeep: mice track smoothly and precisely on the PU leather, reviewers note the surface stays dry-feeling through extended use, and spills wipe off with a damp cloth and mild soap. Reports are equally consistent about the weak point — grip. On a slick desk the mat can slide when a laptop or keyboard is moved, so it performs best on a clean, dry desktop where the backing can hold.

Who This Is For

Laptop-first and small-desk setups that need coverage without depth — at 12.4 inches deep it fits shallow desks that crowd out standard mats — and anyone who wants a clean, finished desk surface for video calls. Satechi rates the material safe for wooden and varnished desks, so it also suits people protecting a good desktop. If you want cushioning, several sizes, or a non-slip base, a thicker mat fits better. Shopping by desk size first? Our desk mat guide for small desks compares this mat with the YSAGi on depth and fit.

Bottom Line

Buy the Satechi for its shallow 12.4-inch depth, its wipe-clean vegan leather finish, and its flat, finished look. Skip it if you need wrist cushioning, a choice of sizes, or a base that grips — those tradeoffs are the whole product.
