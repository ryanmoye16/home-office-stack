What We Liked

The waterproof PU surface is the feature owners mention most: coffee rings, dust, and crumbs wipe off with a damp cloth, and the desk underneath stays protected from scratches and spills.

Stitched edges resist the fraying that unstitched budget mats develop, and the non-slip base keeps the mat planted while you move a mouse or shift a keyboard — both points come up repeatedly in long-term owner reports.

Sizes start at 24x14 in, which covers a keyboard and mouse on a small desk without overhang, and the smooth surface doubles as a writing and mouse-tracking surface.

What Could Be Better

Owners report a slight initial smell from the base that dissipates within a day or two of airing out.

It is thin and firm, with little cushion — if you want padding under your wrists for long writing sessions, a thicker or softer mat fits better.

Some larger sizes arrive with minor factory edge imperfections, and quality control varies; the PU surface also reads as synthetic rather than real leather up close.

Performance in Daily Use

Long-term owner reports describe a mat that keeps looking clean with regular wipe-downs: mouse tracking stays smooth and consistent, edges show no fraying, and the non-slip base remains effective after months of daily use. The recurring caveats are the ones above — the first-day smell and occasional edge imperfections on larger sizes.

Who This Is For

Anyone setting up a home office desk who wants wipe-clean protection in a size that fits — especially small desks, where the 24x14 in starting size covers keyboard and mouse without hanging over the edge. Skip it if you want a thick, cushioned surface.

Bottom Line

For a wipe-clean, stay-put desk surface that makes a desk look put-together immediately, owners rate the YSAGi highly. It is the simple, practical pick in this category — measure your desk first and choose the size that fits it.
