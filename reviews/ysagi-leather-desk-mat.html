Why Get a Desk Mat?

If you have a standing desk or any desk that sees daily use, a desk mat is one of those simple upgrades that has an outsized impact on how the space feels. It protects the desk surface from scratches, coffee rings, and the slow wear of daily use. More importantly, it gives your setup a unified, intentional look instead of just "laptop on a desk."


For mechanical keyboard users, a desk mat also catches keycap lube and desk dust that would otherwise build up on your desk surface. For mouse users, it provides a consistent gliding surface that desk wood or glass can't match.

First Impressions

Out of the package, the YSAGi desk mat has minimal chemical smell — a common issue with cheaper PU leather products. The leather texture is smooth but not glossy, and the stitched border adds a clean visual frame. The rubber base grips the desk immediately; once placed, it doesn't shift.


The 2mm thickness is substantial enough to feel premium but thin enough to sit flush with the desk edge. Unlike thinner mats that curl at the corners, this one lays flat out of the box.

Daily Use

After months of daily use, the YSAGi mat has held up well. The surface shows no signs of peeling or cracking, and the stitched edges remain intact. Cleaning is as easy as wiping with a damp cloth — coffee rings, dust, and minor spills wipe right off.


The mouse tracking is smooth and consistent, which matters if you use an optical or laser mouse without a dedicated mouse pad. If you're a gamer who needs a low-friction surface, this isn't optimized for that — a dedicated gaming mouse pad will outperform it. But for work and general use, it excels.

What Could Be Better

It's PU leather, not the real thing. This means it won't develop the character of a genuine leather desk pad over time — no patina, no softening. That's fine at this price point, but worth noting if you're comparing to $50+ genuine leather options.


At 23.6" x 13.8", it's sized for a single-monitor setup. If you have a ultrawide monitor (34"+) or dual monitors, you'll want the larger size, which YSAGi also sells. Make sure to check dimensions before ordering.

Who It's For

Anyone who wants to upgrade their desk setup without spending much. If your desk looks like "just a desk," a desk mat instantly makes it look like a workspace. Combined with a monitor arm and a laptop stand, a desk mat is the finishing touch that ties the whole setup together. At under $20, there's no reason not to add one.
