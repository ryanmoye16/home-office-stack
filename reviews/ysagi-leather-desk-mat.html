What We Liked

The waterproof surface wipes clean instantly — coffee rings, dust, and crumbs all come off with a damp cloth. Stitched edges mean this won't start fraying after 6 months like cheap mats.

The non-slip base grips the desk reliably. The mat doesn't shift when you move a mouse or adjust a keyboard.

For a large mat, the value is genuinely absurd. Several colleagues bought one within a week of seeing it on my desk.

What Could Be Better

The cork base has a slight initial smell that dissipates within a day or two. Some larger sizes show minor factory edge imperfections — quality control is inconsistent at this price.

The PU leather look is clearly not real leather — but for a desk mat that's actually a feature, not a drawback.

Performance in Daily Use

After 3 months of daily use, the mat looks essentially new. Mouse tracking is smooth and consistent. The edges have not frayed. The non-slip base remains effective.

Who This Is For

Anyone who wants an immediate desk upgrade for under $10. This is the first thing we recommend to anyone setting up a new home office.

Bottom Line

Buy two. One for your desk and one as a backup. The YSAGi leather desk mat is the cheapest way to make any desk look like you have your act together.
