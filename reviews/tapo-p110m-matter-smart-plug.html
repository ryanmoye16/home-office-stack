What We Liked

Matter support means it works across Alexa, Google Assistant, Siri, SmartThings, and Apple Home instead of locking you into one platform. Energy monitoring tracks the use of the connected device and lets you enter your electricity rate for bill estimates in the Tapo app. The compact body is designed to avoid blocking the adjacent outlet. Scheduling, Auto-Off, and Away Mode are built in, and voice control works through Alexa, Siri, or Google Assistant.

What Could Be Better

It is 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only, with Bluetooth used for setup, so band-steered mesh networks can complicate pairing. Matter setup works best with the plug close to your Matter controller for the initial pairing. Energy data is richest in the Tapo app; what carries over into a Matter platform has depended on firmware and platform support, so check there if that is your main use. Two plugs side by side can still feel tight despite the compact design, per owner reports.

Performance in Daily Use

Reviewers and owners consistently praise the energy charts: the Tapo app shows daily, monthly, and yearly use and cost estimates once a rate is entered, and owners report using that data to spot appliances drawing more than expected. Matter reviewers describe QR-code setup as straightforward when the plug starts out near the controller, with on/off control working across whichever platform they paired it to. Scheduling and Auto-Off are the features owners lean on day to day for lamps, fans, and other switch-controlled devices.

Who This Is For

Households building on Matter who want energy monitoring without tying themselves to a single platform, and smart-home beginners who want scheduling and voice control in one plug. If your home is HomeKit-only and you do not need Matter, the TP-Link Kasa EP25 covers that ecosystem with its own energy monitoring; if your home is all Alexa and you need neither Matter nor energy monitoring, the Amazon Smart Plug is the simpler pick.

Bottom Line

The P110M is the cross-platform pick in this lineup: Matter certification plus energy monitoring is the combination the Alexa-only and HomeKit-only plugs do not offer. Buy it for the flexibility and the usage data, and expect to do the deepest energy reporting in the Tapo app rather than in your platform app.
