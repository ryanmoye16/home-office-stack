What We Liked

Straightforward installation with clear instructions, per owner reports. All necessary hardware is included, and both C-clamp and grommet mount options are in the box.

Height, tilt, swivel, and rotation cover the standard ergonomic adjustments, and owners report the arm stays stable once mounted, with no wobble at normal typing speeds.

The integrated cable management channel keeps the desk tidy.

What Could Be Better

At 20 lb max, it won't handle ultrawide monitors or heavy professional displays. Build quality is functional rather than premium, in line with owner expectations for the Amazon Basics line.

The adjustment mechanisms are slightly stiff out of the box, according to owners; they report the arm loosens up after a few days of use.

Performance in Daily Use

Owner reports describe a steady hold on standard 24 to 27 inch monitors, with no drift or sag once the arm is tensioned correctly. The recurring caveat is the break-in period: adjustment feels stiff at first, then smooths out with use.

Who This Is For

Budget-conscious buyers with standard 24 to 27 inch monitors under 20 lb. If you're building a first home office and want the basic adjustments without a gas-spring mechanism, this arm covers the essentials. Running a wider panel? Our ultrawide monitor arm guide runs the weight and headroom numbers on this arm against 34-inch panels — including where its 20 lb capacity and stated 32-inch size maximum become the limits.

Bottom Line

The Amazon Basics Single Monitor Arm is the straightforward budget pick: it holds a standard monitor, adjusts in the four directions that matter, and includes both mounting options. Choose a gas-spring arm instead if you reposition your monitor often.
