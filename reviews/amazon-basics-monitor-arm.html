What We Liked

Easy 5-minute installation with clear instructions. All necessary hardware included — you won't need to make a hardware store run. The arm is smooth and stable once mounted, with no wobble at normal typing speeds.

Height, tilt, swivel, and rotation all work as expected. The cable management channel keeps things tidy. Both C-clamp and grommet mount options are included.

What Could Be Better

At 20 lbs max, it won't handle ultrawave monitors or heavy professional displays. Build quality is what you'd expect from Amazon Basics — functional but not premium.

The adjustment mechanisms are slightly stiff out of the box. A few days of use loosens them up.

Performance in Daily Use

Steady hold on a 27-inch Dell Ultrasharp (14 lbs) over 2 months. No drift, no sag. The arm does what it's supposed to do.

Who This Is For

Budget-conscious buyers with standard 24-27 inch monitors under 20 lbs. If you're building a first home office on a budget, this arm won't let you down.

Bottom Line

This is the monitor arm to buy if you're not trying to spend $150 on a fully-adjustable professional arm. It works, it's stable, and it gets the job done.
