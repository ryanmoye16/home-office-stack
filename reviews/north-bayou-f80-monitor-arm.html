What We Liked

The gas spring mechanism is genuinely smooth — adjusting monitor height takes barely any force, and the arm holds position perfectly once set. This is the key differentiator from cheaper friction-based arms.

22,675 reviews at 4.6 stars is an extraordinary track record. When a product sells this many units and maintains this rating, it's a proven winner.

Full motion adjustability — height, tilt, swivel, rotation, and pan — covers every ergonomic need. Both C-clamp and grommet mount options included.

At $29.90, the value is exceptional. This is the budget monitor arm that doesn't feel like a budget product.

What Could Be Better

Max weight of 19.8 lbs won't handle ultra-heavy professional monitors (like the Apple Studio Display or Dell U3223QE). For those, you'll need a heavier-duty arm.

The assembly instructions are minimal — plan to spend 15-20 minutes on installation and have a helper for the final monitor attachment step.

Plastic cable clips are included but feel flimsy — upgrade to Velcro ties for a cleaner look.

Performance in Daily Use

Steady hold on a 27-inch Dell Ultrasharp (14 lbs) over 2 months of daily use. No drift, no sag. The gas spring adjustment is buttery smooth compared to friction-based arms at this price. The arm doesn't wobble during typing.
