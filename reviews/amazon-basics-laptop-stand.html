What We Liked

Six height positions cover the range from flat to a comfortable typing tilt. The aluminum build is solid — no wobble or flex at normal typing speeds. Folding flat makes it easy to slip into a laptop bag.

The price makes this a no-brainer. There's no decision to make — it's the default laptop stand recommendation.

What Could Be Better

No storage space underneath for a keyboard or notepad when the laptop is raised. Some laptops with bottom-ventilation might benefit from a more open design, but this hasn't been an issue in testing.

Max weight of 10 lbs means it's not suitable for heavy 16-inch professional laptops like the MacBook Pro 16.

Performance in Daily Use

Steady hold on a 13-inch MacBook Air (2.7 lbs) and a 14-inch Dell XPS (3.5 lbs) across all height positions. Folds and unfolds in about 3 seconds.

Who This Is For

Anyone who uses a laptop at a desk and wants to raise the screen to eye level. The fold-flat design makes it equally suitable for home offices and travel.

Bottom Line

There's no reason to look elsewhere. The Amazon Basics Laptop Stand is stable, adjustable, portable, and cheap. Buy it.
