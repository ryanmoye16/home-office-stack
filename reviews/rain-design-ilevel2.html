What We Liked

The height adjustment mechanism is genuinely clever — a front-facing slider that lets you raise or lower the screen with one hand while the laptop is on the stand. Most laptop stands require you to remove the laptop, adjust, then replace it. The iLevel 2's slider means you can adjust on the fly during a workday.

The aluminum build is premium. It feels solid and stable, with rubber pads that grip the desk and protect your laptop. Heat dissipation from the aluminum body is excellent — the laptop stays cool even under sustained use.

The design aesthetic matches Apple hardware well. If you have a MacBook, this stand looks like it belongs on your desk rather than being an afterthought.

What Could Be Better

Not foldable — this is a desk-only stand. If you travel frequently, the lack of fold-flat portability is a real drawback. The Rain Design mStand might be a better choice for travelers.

It's premium-priced for a static-geometry stand. You're paying for the patented mechanism and aluminum build, not for versatility.

The lack of VESA mounting means it won't work with external monitor arm setups.

Performance in Daily Use

Tested with a 14-inch MacBook Pro over two weeks. The height slider mechanism worked reliably every time — smooth, no resistance, no slippage once set. The aluminum body dissipates heat effectively; the laptop bottom stayed at ambient temperature even after 3 hours of use.

The open design means no heat trapping, which is especially important for M-series MacBooks that throttle performance when hot.

Who This Is For

MacBook and ultrabook users who want a premium, desk-bound stand with effortless height adjustment. If you frequently change your working position — sitting to standing, or just want to raise the screen for a video call — the iLevel 2's slider mechanism is worth the premium over fixed stands.

Bottom Line

The Rain Design iLevel 2 is the best laptop stand you can buy if you want premium build quality and effortless adjustability. Wirecutter's #1 pick for good reason. The patented slider mechanism alone justifies the price over fixed alternatives.
