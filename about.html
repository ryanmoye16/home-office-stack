About Us

Your Home Office, Sorted.

Budget Tech Picks is the home office resource for remote workers — honest reviews of the physical desk gear and the software tools that actually make working from home work.

🖥️ Desk Gear

Hands-on reviews of monitor arms, USB-C hubs, smart plugs, keyboards, and the accessories that make a desk actually work. Every product tested in a real home office over weeks — not just unboxed and spec-sheet reviewed.

⚙️ Work Tools

Reviews of the software platforms remote workers actually use — email marketing tools, course platforms, web hosting, and productivity software. We cover the tools and tell you which ones pay affiliates to refer them.

How We Review

For desk gear: we buy products with our own money or receive them from manufacturers with no obligation to review or recommend. Each review is based on extended real-world use — we use the products daily for weeks and report what we actually experience.

For software tools: we verify features, compare pricing, and test affiliate program terms. We only recommend platforms we'd use ourselves or that we verify are well-regarded by creators we trust.

Our Principles

✓

No sponsored reviews

We never accept payment for reviews. If a brand sends us a product, it gets the same treatment as one we bought ourselves.

✓

Real-world testing

We use every product for at least two weeks before publishing a review. No 24-hour hot takes.

✓

Honest pros and cons

We tell you what's wrong with a product alongside what's right. A 4-star review means something different than a 5-star.

✓

Budget-first, not cheap

We focus on value — gear and tools that earn their keep. Not the cheapest thing, not the most expensive. The one that's actually worth what it costs.
