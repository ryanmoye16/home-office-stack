About Us

Your Home Office, Sorted.

Home Office Stack is the home office resource for remote workers — honest reviews of the physical desk gear and the software tools that actually make working from home work.

🖥️ Desk Gear

Research-based reviews of monitor arms, USB-C hubs, smart plugs, keyboards, and the accessories that make a desk actually work. Every review is grounded in manufacturer specs and real owner feedback — not just a spec-sheet summary.

⚙️ Work Tools

Reviews of the software platforms remote workers actually use — email marketing tools, course platforms, web hosting, and productivity software. We compare features, pricing, and user feedback to recommend what actually works for the home office setup.

How We Review

For desk gear: each review is research-based — grounded in manufacturer specs and published owner feedback from people who use the product daily. We report what owners actually experience, including the complaints, and we never claim hands-on testing we haven't done.

For software tools: we verify features and compare pricing against each platform's own published terms, and weigh published user and creator feedback. We only recommend tools that hold up on features, terms, and real user reports.

The full rule sheet — where facts come from, what we refuse to invent, how ratings are dated, and how corrections are made — is on our How We Research page.

Our Principles

✓

No sponsored reviews

We never accept payment for reviews or placement. Every product gets the same research-based treatment, whether a brand contacts us or not.

✓

Grounded in owner feedback

Every review is built from manufacturer specs and published owner feedback — the patterns that only show up after weeks of daily use, not 24-hour hot takes.

✓

Honest pros and cons

We tell you what's wrong with a product alongside what's right. A 4-star review means something different than a 5-star.

✓

Smart value, not just cheap

We focus on gear and tools that earn their keep — the ones that actually make your home office work better. Not the cheapest, not the most expensive. The one that's actually worth it.
