Why Course Platforms?

If you have skills, knowledge, or experience that others would pay to learn — a course platform is how you monetize it at scale. One piece of content, created once, sold an unlimited number of times. Unlike consulting or freelance work, a course doesn't require your time for every sale.

For remote workers specifically, course creation is often the first real income diversification step. You already work from home. You already have expertise in something. A course platform turns that into a product.

Teachable — Best Overall

30% recurring for 12 months — Avg affiliate earns $450/month

Teachable is the platform that's taken over the creator economy for course selling. It's the platform you'll see recommended most often by course creators, and for good reason — it handles everything from payments to course delivery to email marketing in one clean dashboard.

The free plan lets you start without paying anything. The Professional plan ($119/month) removes Teachable's transaction fee and is what serious course creators use. You keep almost all of the revenue.

Affiliate note: Teachable's affiliate program pays 30% recurring for the first year per referred customer. The average affiliate earns $450/month. Top affiliates earn over $1,000/month. Approval is straightforward — apply at teachable.com/partners.

Thinkific — Best Free Option

30% recurring for 12 months

Thinkific competes directly with Teachable and wins on price. Their Free plan lets you build and sell courses with zero monthly cost — Teachable's free plan takes a transaction fee. The Basic plan ($49/month) is what most course creators need.

What sets Thinkific apart: it's arguably easier to use for first-time course builders. The course editor is intuitive, the website builder is solid, and you can white-label your course site without upgrading. Good for creators who want full control over the student experience.

Affiliate note: Thinkific affiliates earn 30% recurring for 12 months. Less widely promoted than Teachable, which means less competition as an affiliate.

NO APPLICATION — 60% lifetime recurring

Systeme.io is the platform that replaces your entire software stack. Course hosting, email marketing, sales funnels, and websites — all in one $27/month tool. If you're building a course business from scratch, this is the fastest and cheapest way to get everything running.
