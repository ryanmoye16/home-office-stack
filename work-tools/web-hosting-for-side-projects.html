Why Web Hosting Matters for Remote Workers

Every remote worker building a personal brand, a freelance portfolio, a blog, or an online business needs a website. That website needs hosting. It's one of the most reliable affiliate products because everyone who builds anything online eventually needs it.

The hosting programs below are ranked two ways: as products we'd recommend to someone building a site, and as affiliate programs we'd promote for income. Some hosting companies pay $65/sale. Others pay $500/sale. The difference is enormous for your revenue.

Systeme.io isn't traditional web hosting — it's an all-in-one business platform that includes website building, email marketing, course hosting, and sales funnels. But if you're a remote worker launching a side project or online business, it might be the only tool you need.

Hostinger — Best Cheap Hosting for Portfolios

40-60% per sale ($50-200/sale)

For a portfolio, blog, or small business site, Hostinger's Basic plan ($2.99/month) is more than enough. For anything requiring serious resources, their cloud hosting starts at $9.99/month with better specs.
