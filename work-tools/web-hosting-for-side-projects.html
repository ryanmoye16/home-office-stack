Why Web Hosting Matters for Remote Workers

Every remote worker building a personal brand, a freelance portfolio, a blog, or an online business needs a website. That website needs hosting. It's one of the most foundational tools for anyone building an online presence.

The hosting options below are ranked as products we'd recommend to someone building a site. We focused on price, performance, ease of use, and what each host is actually good at.

Systeme.io — Best Value (Hosting + Business Tools)

Systeme.io isn't traditional web hosting — it's an all-in-one business platform that includes website building, email marketing, course hosting, and sales funnels. But if you're a remote worker launching a side project or online business, it might be the only tool you need.

Plans start at $27/month and include unlimited email sending, 3 sales funnels, and course hosting for up to 5 courses. That's cheaper than buying separate hosting ($5-10/month), email marketing ($15-25/month), and course software ($29-119/month).

The catch: Systeme.io isn't a replacement for a full-featured website builder like WordPress with a quality host. If you need full control over your site, look at Hostinger instead.

Visit Systeme.io →

Hostinger — Best Cheap Hosting for Portfolios

Hostinger is the budget hosting provider that's actually good. Their $2.99/month shared hosting plan is the cheapest entry point in the industry, and their performance consistently beats hosts at double the price. The control panel (hPanel) is clean and beginner-friendly.

For a portfolio, blog, or small business site, Hostinger's Basic plan ($2.99/month) is more than enough. For anything requiring serious resources, their cloud hosting starts at $9.99/month with better specs.

The trade-off: Hostinger's cheapest plan has limits (only 1 website, no domain), and support can be slow since it's budget-tier service. Uptime is generally excellent but not guaranteed at the enterprise level.

Visit Hostinger.com →

Liquid Web — Best for Mission-Critical Sites

Liquid Web is managed hosting for serious projects — WooCommerce stores, agency sites, high-traffic blogs, and businesses that need guaranteed uptime and support. Not the cheapest, but consistently rated the best for mission-critical sites.

Plans start at $27/month for managed WordPress hosting, with VPS and dedicated server options for larger projects. The trade-off: this isn't portfolio hosting. It's for clients or projects where reliability is non-negotiable.

The downside for most remote workers: Liquid Web's pricing is overkill for personal projects, portfolios, or side-hustle sites. The entry-level plans are fine, but you quickly outgrow shared-hosting pricing.

Visit LiquidWeb.com →

Bottom Line

For Most Side Projects: Hostinger. For Full Businesses: Systeme.io.

For a simple portfolio, blog, or freelance site, Hostinger at $2.99/month is the clear winner — cheap and reliable. If you're building a more ambitious side business that needs email, courses, and funnels alongside hosting, Systeme.io's $27/month bundle is the better value.

Liquid Web is worth considering if you're running a high-traffic site or client work where uptime and support matter more than price.
