Email Marketing for Remote Workers

The best email marketing platforms for freelancers and remote workers building an audience — ranked by price, features, and ease of use.

Updated May 2026

Why Email Marketing for Remote Workers?

If you're working from home and want to build a real, sustainable income — email marketing is the foundation. Not social media followers who disappear when the algorithm changes. Not views that evaporate overnight. A list you own, that pays you every month.

The platforms below are the ones we recommend for remote workers specifically. They're tools we've used ourselves or verified work well for freelancers, creators, and side-project builders.

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — Best for Creators

Kit is the email marketing platform built specifically for creators — bloggers, YouTubers, podcasters, and course sellers who want to grow an audience without wrestling with complex software. The free plan gets you started, and the Creator Plan ($25/month for up to 1,000 subscribers) has everything most freelancers need.

What makes Kit stand out for remote workers is its simplicity and its visual automation builder. You can set up a welcome sequence, a sales funnel, or a newsletter in under an hour. No developer required.

The main downside: Kit is pricier than some competitors once you grow past 1,000 subscribers, and its template selection is more limited than GetResponse.

Visit Kit.com →

GetResponse — Best All-in-One Marketing Suite

GetResponse is the platform that's been quietly winning over freelancers and small businesses for years. It started as an email tool and grew into a full marketing suite — email marketing, automation, landing pages, webinars, and even an AI email writer. At $19/month for the Basic plan, it's also one of the most affordable options that doesn't feel entry-level.

What remote workers love: the pre-built funnels and newsletter templates. You can go from zero to a functional email list in an afternoon. The AI recommendation tool helps you build subject lines and body copy that actually converts.

The trade-off: GetResponse has a steeper learning curve than Kit, and some users find the interface dated compared to newer platforms.

Visit GetResponse.com →

Systeme.io — Best Value for Side Hustles

Systeme.io is the platform that makes building an online business feel possible. It combines email marketing, sales funnels, course hosting, and website building in one $27/month tool. No transaction fees. No per-email costs. Just a flat price that doesn't punish you for growing.

For remote workers starting a side project — a course, a coaching offer, a newsletter with paid subscriptions — Systeme.io is the fastest path from zero to launched. The free plan exists, and the paid plans start at $27/month.

The catch: Systeme.io isn't as polished as dedicated email tools. If you only need email marketing and nothing else, it may be overkill at $27/month when Kit's free plan exists.

Visit Systeme.io →

Which Platform Should You Choose?

New to email marketing? → Start with Systeme.io

Instant approval, lifetime flat pricing, and it doubles as your course platform and funnel builder. $27/month covers everything. Visit Systeme.io →

Building a creator-focused newsletter? → Kit

Built specifically for creators. Best-in-class sender reputation and creator community. Visit Kit.com →

Want a full marketing suite? → GetResponse

Email, automation, landing pages, webinars, and AI writing tools — one platform. Visit GetResponse.com →

Bottom Line

Start with Systeme.io

For remote workers building a side income, Systeme.io is the best entry point. $27/month gets you email marketing, a website, course hosting, and sales funnels — one tool instead of four. It's the fastest path from zero to launched.

If you're specifically building a creator-focused newsletter and want something more polished, Kit is the better choice for that use case.
