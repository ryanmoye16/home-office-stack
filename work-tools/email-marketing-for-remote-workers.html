Why Email Marketing for Remote Workers?

If you're working from home and want to build a real, sustainable income — email marketing is the foundation. Not social media followers who disappear when the algorithm changes. Not views that evaporate overnight. A list you own, that pays you every month.

The platforms below are the ones we recommend for remote workers specifically. Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — Best for Creators

50% recurring for 12 months

Kit is the email marketing platform built specifically for creators — bloggers, YouTubers, podcasters, and course sellers who want to grow an audience without wrestling with complex software. The free plan gets you started, and the Creator Plan ($25/month for up to 1,000 subscribers) has everything most freelancers need.

What makes Kit stand out for remote workers is its simplicity and its visual automation builder. You can set up a welcome sequence, a sales funnel, or a newsletter in under an hour. No developer required.

GetResponse — Best All-in-One Marketing Suite

33-60% recurring for 12 months

GetResponse is the platform that's been quietly winning over freelancers and small businesses for years. It started as an email tool and grew into a full marketing suite — email marketing, automation, landing pages, webinars, and even an AI email writer. At $19/month for the Basic plan, it's also one of the most affordable options that doesn't feel entry-level.

What remote workers love: the pre-built funnels and newsletter templates. You can go from zero to a functional email list in an afternoon. The AI recommendation tool helps you build subject lines and body copy that actually converts.
